Online reputation management (ORM) services shape how your business appears across search results, reviews, and social platforms — and increasingly, how AI systems describe you when someone asks for a recommendation in your category. An ORM company monitors what is said about you, responds to it, and builds the accurate, positive record that should be found first. This guide covers what the work includes, how ORM and SEO fit together, what it costs in India, and what no honest provider can promise.

## What is online reputation management?

Online reputation management is the ongoing work of monitoring and influencing what people find when they look you up. That covers the first page of Google for your brand name, your star ratings on Google and industry review platforms, what is said on social media and forums, and how AI assistants summarize you. The aim is an accurate public record, not a spotless one. A business with a 4.6 rating and thoughtful replies to its critics is more credible than one with a suspicious wall of five-star reviews.

## What does an ORM company actually do?

A solid ORM program includes review monitoring and response across Google and industry-specific platforms, proactive positive content generation, and monitoring of brand mentions across the web to catch and address issues early.

In practice the work falls into six parts:

| Activity | What it involves |
| --- | --- |
| Reputation audit | Recording what appears for your brand name in Google, on review platforms and in AI answers |
| Monitoring | Tracking new reviews and mentions so nothing sits unanswered |
| Review response | Replying to positive and negative reviews promptly, specifically and in your voice |
| Review generation | Asking real customers for reviews at the right moment, within each platform's rules |
| Content and search | Publishing and optimizing pages you control so they rank for your name |
| Listing accuracy | Correcting wrong names, addresses, phone numbers, hours and descriptions wherever they appear |

## What is included in ORM services?

An engagement usually starts with the audit and a response plan, then settles into a monthly cycle: monitor, respond, request reviews, publish, and report. A good report shows your average rating and review count over time, the share of reviews answered, what occupies page one for your brand name, and how AI assistants describe you this month compared with last. If a provider reports only activity, such as posts published or profiles created, you cannot tell whether your reputation moved.

## How do ORM and SEO work together?

ORM borrows SEO's tools and points them at a different target. Ordinary SEO tries to rank your pages for the keywords buyers search. ORM uses the same methods to control what ranks for your own name. The pages that usually can be made to rank there are your website, your Google Business Profile, your LinkedIn and other social profiles, listings on credible directories and review platforms, and press or guest articles about you.

When those results are strong, complete and current, an outdated article or an unrepresentative complaint has less room on page one. That is what "suppression" means when it is done properly: outranking a weak result with better ones, not hiding anything. It works for stale or marginal content. It does not bury a serious, well-sourced story, and it should not be sold as if it could. See our [SEO services](/services/seo) for the underlying work.

## Why does reputation now affect AI-generated answers too?

Generative AI models often draw on aggregated sentiment and third-party content when describing a business, which means inconsistent information or unaddressed negative reviews can shape how ChatGPT or Perplexity characterize your brand — tying ORM directly to our [GEO services](/services/geo-services).

This has raised the stakes. A searcher on Google sees ten results and forms a view. A person asking an assistant gets one paragraph, written from whatever the model found, and may never see the sources. If your listings disagree with each other, or the most detailed thing written about you is a complaint, that is what the summary reflects. Fixing the public record is now part of being described correctly by AI, which is why we treat ORM and [Generative Engine Optimization](/blog/what-is-geo) as one job.

## What can't ORM do?

Be wary of any provider that promises the following.

- **Removing genuine reviews.** Platforms remove reviews that break their rules: spam, fakes, conflicts of interest, the wrong business. A real customer's honest complaint stays.
- **Buying or writing fake reviews.** This violates platform policies, can get a listing penalized, and misleads customers. It is also the fastest way to turn a small problem into a public one.
- **Guaranteed deletion of news or court records.** Legitimate reporting and public records are rarely removable. Where content is defamatory or unlawful, that is a legal matter for a lawyer, not a marketing task.
- **Instant results.** Reputation changes as new reviews and coverage accumulate. Anyone promising a fix in a week is promising something else.

## How much do ORM services cost in India?

There is no standard price, because scope varies so widely. Public listings on [IndiaMART](https://m.indiamart.com/impcat/reputation-management-services.html) show Indian providers quoting from about ₹5,000 to ₹90,000 a month. The lower end generally means review monitoring and replies for a single location. The higher end means multi-location brands, content production, search work and crisis response. What moves the price is the number of locations and platforms, the volume of reviews, how much content has to be produced, and whether you are maintaining a good reputation or repairing a damaged one.

## How long does ORM take to show results?

Meaningful, sustained reputation improvement typically takes 3 to 6 months. Review replies and listing corrections show up within days. A rating moves more slowly, because it is an average and needs a steady stream of new reviews to shift. Search results for your brand name take longest, since new and improved pages have to be crawled and earn their place.

## How do you choose an ORM company?

Ask how they generate reviews, and walk away if the answer involves paying for them or writing them. Ask what they will not do. Ask to see a sample monthly report. Ask whether they cover AI answers as well as Google and review sites. And check their own reputation: an ORM company with thin reviews and an out-of-date profile is showing you its work.

## How is ORM different from PR?

PR focuses on proactively generating positive coverage and media relationships, while ORM is more defensive and monitoring-focused — though the two work best together for businesses serious about their public perception.

## Can you manage online reputation yourself?

A single-location business can do the essentials in-house. Claim and complete your Google Business Profile, turn on review notifications, reply to every review within a couple of days, and ask satisfied customers for a review as part of your normal process. Bring in a company when you have several locations, a volume of reviews you cannot keep up with, a specific incident to recover from, or a brand-name search result you need to outrank.
