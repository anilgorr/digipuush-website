Facebook ads in India cost roughly ₹4 to ₹15 per click for most businesses in 2026, and ₹15 to ₹45 or more in competitive categories such as B2B, finance and real estate. Typical CPM runs from about ₹40 to ₹250 per 1,000 impressions, and a workable starting budget is ₹20,000 to ₹30,000 a month. This guide gives the benchmarks by industry and objective, explains why published figures disagree so much, and shows how to estimate your own cost before you spend.

## How much do Facebook ads cost in India in 2026?

There is no rate card. Meta sells ads through an auction, so the price changes with who you target, what you ask the campaign to optimize for, and how good your creative is. These are the ranges that recent India benchmarks agree on:

| Metric | Typical range in India (2026) | What pushes it higher |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) | ₹40 – ₹250 | Narrow audiences, conversion objectives, festive season |
| CPC (cost per link click), most businesses | ₹4 – ₹15 | Weak creative, low click-through rate |
| CPC, B2B, finance, real estate | ₹15 – ₹45+ | Senior job titles, high-value leads |
| Cost per lead | ₹100 – ₹2,000 | Industry, form length, landing page quality |

A practitioner who published figures from ₹13.4 crore of managed spend [reported](https://ansaridanish.com/facebook-ads-cost-india/) CPMs between ₹44 and ₹104 and CPCs between ₹4.71 and ₹14.96 across three consumer accounts, with a blended CPC near ₹7. That is a useful sanity check, because it comes from real accounts rather than estimates.

## Why do published Facebook ad cost figures vary so much?

You will find guides quoting ₹0.50 a click and others quoting ₹50. Both can be true, because they measure different things. Three reasons account for most of the gap.

**The objective.** A traffic or engagement campaign buys the cheapest clicks available, often under ₹3, from people unlikely to buy. A leads or sales campaign pays more per click because Meta is bidding for people likely to convert.

**The definition of a click.** "CPC (all)" counts every tap on the ad, including likes and profile views. "CPC (link click)" counts only people who went to your site. The first number is always lower.

**The audience.** A broad audience across tier-2 and tier-3 cities costs a fraction of what it costs to reach founders or finance heads in metros. One 2026 benchmark [puts](https://paidmediaworld.com/cost-of-facebook-ads-in-india-guide-benchmarks-for-leads-sales/) CPM at about ₹40 for general tier-3 targeting and up to ₹650 for high-intent B2B groups.

So compare like with like. The figure that matters for budgeting is cost per link click on a conversion-optimized campaign, aimed at the audience you actually sell to.

| Industry | Typical CPC (link click) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E-commerce and D2C | ₹3 – ₹15 | Broad audiences and visual products keep costs low |
| Restaurants and local services | ₹2 – ₹10 | Small geographic radius, simple offers |
| Education and coaching | ₹5 – ₹20 | Seasonal spikes around admissions |
| Healthcare and clinics | ₹6 – ₹18 | Ad policy limits targeting and claims |
| Real estate | ₹10 – ₹40 | High value per lead, heavy competition |
| Finance and insurance | ₹12 – ₹40 | Among the most contested audiences |
| B2B and SaaS | ₹15 – ₹45+ | Narrow, senior audiences; costs rise with seniority |

These are ranges compiled from published 2026 India benchmarks, including [upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/facebook-advertising-pricing/), [Paid Media World](https://paidmediaworld.com/cost-of-facebook-ads-in-india-guide-benchmarks-for-leads-sales/) and [Town Media Labs](https://www.townmedialabs.com/blog/facebook-instagram-ads-cost-india). Treat them as a starting assumption, then replace them with your own data after two weeks of spend.

E-commerce and D2C brands typically see the most efficient costs due to broad, engaged audiences, while B2B and financial services categories face higher costs per click and per lead due to narrower, more competitive targeting.

## What determines Facebook ad costs in India?

Cost is driven primarily by audience competition (how many advertisers are bidding for the same audience), campaign objective (awareness campaigns cost less per impression than conversion campaigns), and creative quality and relevance score.

Two more factors matter in India specifically. Seasonality is sharp: CPMs climb through the festive quarter, when every consumer brand raises its budget at once. And placement changes the price. Instagram placements usually cost more per click than Facebook placements, with one India benchmark putting the gap at 30% to 50%.

## How do you calculate Facebook ad cost for your business?

You do not need a tool. Three lines of arithmetic give you a forecast.

1. **Clicks** = monthly budget ÷ cost per click
2. **Leads** = clicks × landing page conversion rate
3. **Cost per lead** = monthly budget ÷ leads

A worked example: ₹30,000 a month at ₹10 a click buys 3,000 clicks. If 3% of visitors fill in your form, that is 90 leads at about ₹333 each. Lift the conversion rate to 5% and the same spend produces 150 leads at ₹200 each, without touching the ads.

If you only know CPM, convert it: CPC = CPM ÷ (1,000 × click-through rate). A ₹150 CPM at a 1.5% click-through rate works out to ₹10 a click.

## What is the minimum budget for Facebook ads in India?

Meta's platform minimum is low and varies by objective; Ads Manager tells you if a budget is under the floor. The practical minimum is higher. We recommend ₹20,000 to ₹30,000 a month in ad spend, because Meta's delivery system needs a steady flow of conversions before it optimizes well, and a budget too small to produce them never leaves the learning phase. Below roughly ₹10,000 a month, treat the spend as a test of creative and offer rather than a lead engine.

| Monthly ad spend | What it is good for |
| --- | --- |
| Under ₹10,000 | Testing creative and offers |
| ₹20,000 – ₹30,000 | A single local or niche lead campaign |
| ₹50,000 – ₹1,00,000 | Lead generation or e-commerce with ongoing creative testing |
| ₹1,00,000+ | Multi-campaign funnels with retargeting |

## Is GST charged on Facebook ads in India?

Yes. Advertising services in India [attract 18% GST](https://taxclue.in/guide/gst-on-advertising), so a ₹30,000 media budget costs closer to ₹35,400 in cash terms. If your business is GST-registered and the ads are for business purposes, you can generally claim the tax back as input tax credit. How the tax is billed depends on how your ad account is set up, so check the treatment with your accountant.

## How can businesses reduce their cost per lead?

The most reliable lever is creative and landing page quality — well-targeted campaigns with weak landing pages waste spend on clicks that don't convert. Pairing paid campaigns with strong [website development](/services/website-development) consistently lowers cost per lead more than adjusting bids alone.

Beyond that, four habits make a measurable difference:

- **Test creative constantly.** A better hook lifts click-through rate, and a higher click-through rate lowers CPC directly.
- **Optimize for the result you want.** Choose leads or purchases as the objective, not traffic, even though each click costs more.
- **Keep audiences broad enough.** Over-narrow targeting raises CPM and starves the algorithm of data.
- **Retarget.** People who have already visited your site convert at a far higher rate than cold audiences.
