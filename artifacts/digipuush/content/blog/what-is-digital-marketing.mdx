Digital marketing is the practice of promoting a business through online channels: search engines, social media, paid advertising, email, and now AI answer engines such as ChatGPT and Perplexity. In India in 2026 it is the main way businesses are found, compared and contacted, and that umbrella has expanded well beyond Google search. This guide explains each channel, what it costs in India, and how to decide where your budget goes first.

## What is digital marketing?

Digital marketing is every activity a business uses to reach and convert customers online. It differs from traditional marketing in three ways. It is measurable, because you can see how many people saw, clicked and bought. It is targeted, because you can reach a specific audience instead of everyone who passes a hoarding. And it is adjustable, because a campaign that is not working can be changed the same day.

## What channels make up digital marketing today?

The core channels are search engine optimization (SEO) for Google rankings, Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) for AI visibility, social media marketing, paid advertising, and email marketing. Most effective strategies combine several of these rather than relying on one.

| Channel | What it does | Best for | Time to results |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [SEO](/services/seo) | Ranks your pages in Google's organic results | Steady, compounding inbound demand | 3–6 months |
| [AEO](/services/aeo-services) | Gets your brand cited as the answer in AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity | Buyers who research with AI assistants | 6–12 weeks for first citations |
| [GEO](/services/geo-services) | Shapes how generative AI describes and recommends your brand | Competitive categories where AI shortlists options | 8–16 weeks for first mentions |
| [Paid advertising](/services/paid-marketing) | Buys visibility on Google and Meta | Immediate leads and sales | Days |
| [Social media marketing](/services/social-media-marketing) | Builds an audience on Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and others | Brand awareness and trust | 2–3 months |
| Email and WhatsApp | Speaks directly to people who already know you | Repeat sales and retention | Immediate |
| [Website](/services/website-development) | Converts the traffic every other channel sends | Everything; it is the foundation | Immediate once live |

## How is digital marketing in India different?

The channels are the same everywhere. How they behave in India is not.

**It is mobile-first.** Most Indian users browse, search and buy on a phone. A site that is slow or awkward on mobile wastes whatever you spend sending people to it.

**It is multilingual.** A large share of search and video consumption happens in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi and other Indian languages. A brand that only publishes in English is invisible to part of its market.

**WhatsApp is a sales channel.** Many Indian customers would rather message than fill in a form or make a call. Click-to-WhatsApp ads and quick replies are often the shortest path from interest to enquiry.

**Clicks are cheap, attention is not.** Advertising costs per click are low by global standards, which makes testing affordable. Our guide to [Facebook ads cost in India](/blog/facebook-ads-cost-india) has the current benchmarks. Low prices also mean crowded feeds, so creative quality decides who gets noticed.

**Local search matters.** For any business serving a city or neighbourhood, a complete Google Business Profile with real reviews often drives more enquiries than the website itself.

As more research shifts to conversational AI tools, being cited inside a ChatGPT or Perplexity answer has become as valuable as ranking on page one of Google — sometimes more so, because it's often the only answer the user sees. Read more in our guide to [AEO services](/services/aeo-services).

This is the biggest change to the field in a decade. A buyer who asks an assistant "which agency should I use for X" gets three or four names, not ten links. ## How should Indian businesses prioritize their digital marketing budget?

Most businesses benefit from starting with a strong website and SEO foundation, then layering in AEO/GEO for AI visibility and paid marketing for immediate lead flow — the exact mix depends on your industry and sales cycle. Our [pricing page](/pricing) breaks down typical package options.

A practical order for most:

1. **Fix the website first.** Every channel sends people here. If it is slow, unclear or hard to use on a phone, nothing else pays back.
2. **Claim what is free.** Set up and complete your Google Business Profile, and make your name, address and phone number identical everywhere.
3. **Start SEO early.** It takes months to compound, so the sooner it begins the sooner it pays.
4. **Use paid ads for speed.** When you need leads this month, paid is the only channel that delivers in days.
5. **Add AEO and GEO.** Once your core pages are solid, restructure them so AI engines can cite you.
6. **Build an owned audience.** Email and WhatsApp lists cost almost nothing to reach and belong to you, not to a platform.

By business type: a local service business should weight Google Business Profile, local SEO and click-to-WhatsApp ads. A D2C brand should weight Meta ads, [e-commerce SEO](/ecommerce-seo-services) and retention. A B2B or SaaS company should weight SEO, AEO and GEO, and LinkedIn, because its buyers research for weeks before they talk to anyone.

## How do you measure digital marketing?

Measure outcomes, not activity. The numbers that matter are leads or sales, cost per lead or per sale, and revenue traced back to each channel. Rankings, impressions, followers and AI citations are leading indicators: worth tracking because they predict the outcomes, but not the goal. A report that lists posts published and keywords tracked without saying what they produced is reporting effort.

## Should you do digital marketing in-house or hire an agency?

Keep in-house what depends on knowing your business closely: your positioning, your offers, your customer conversations. Hire out what depends on specialist skill and constant practice: technical SEO, paid media management, and AI visibility work. Many Indian businesses run a hybrid, with one internal marketer coordinating an agency. If you go the agency route, ask for published pricing, a defined scope, and reporting tied to leads and revenue.
