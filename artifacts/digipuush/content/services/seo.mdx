SEO is the practice of optimizing your website's technical structure, content, and authority so it ranks higher on Google for the searches your customers actually run. Digipuush combines technical audits, on-page optimization, and backlink building to grow organic traffic for Indian businesses.

## What's included in Digipuush's SEO service?

We start with a full technical audit covering site speed, mobile usability, crawlability, and indexing issues, then move to on-page optimization — titles, meta descriptions, header structure, internal linking — across your priority pages. We pair this with keyword research grounded in real search volume and intent, and a backlink strategy focused on relevant, authoritative domains rather than volume.

## How is Digipuush's SEO different from a generic SEO agency?

Every SEO engagement we run is built on the same technical foundation as our [AEO](/services/aeo-services) and [GEO](/services/geo-services) work, which means your content stays optimized for AI answer engines as a byproduct of good SEO — not as a separate, disconnected effort.

## How does Digipuush's SEO process work?

1. **Technical audit.** We check crawlability, indexing, site speed, mobile usability and structured data, and fix what blocks Google from reading your pages.
2. **Keyword research.** We map the searches your customers actually run to the pages that should rank for them, by intent and by realistic difficulty.
3. **On-page optimization.** Titles, meta descriptions, headings, internal links and content are rewritten for your priority pages.
4. **Content.** We fill the gaps the keyword map exposes with new pages written to answer the query directly.
5. **Link building.** We earn backlinks from relevant, authoritative sites.
6. **Reporting.** Each month you see ranking movement, organic traffic and what is planned next.

## How long does SEO take to show results?

Most Digipuush clients see measurable ranking improvements within 3 to 4 months, with significant organic traffic growth by month 6. The pace depends on how competitive your category is and the condition your website starts in. A site with serious technical problems often moves fastest, because fixing them unlocks pages Google was already willing to rank.

## What should affordable SEO services in India include?

Price alone tells you little. A low-cost retainer is good value only if it still covers the technical audit, real keyword research, on-page work, some authority building and a monthly report you can verify. Be wary of three things: guaranteed number-one rankings, which no agency can promise; link packages sold by volume; and reports that list activity instead of rankings and traffic. Affordable SEO is SEO where every rupee goes into work that moves those two numbers.
