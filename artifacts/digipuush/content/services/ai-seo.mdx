## What are AI SEO services?

AI SEO services are the work of making a website visible wherever people now search: in Google's ranked results, in the AI Overview above them, and inside the answers written by assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. It combines three disciplines. SEO earns rankings. [AEO](/services/aeo-services) earns citations as the direct answer to a question. [GEO](/services/geo-services) shapes how generative engines describe and recommend your brand. An AI SEO company delivers all three from one plan.

The term is sometimes used for something else, namely using AI tools to produce SEO content faster. That is a production method, not a strategy. AI SEO here means optimizing for AI search.

## How does the AI SEO process work?

1. **Audit.** We review technical health, current Google rankings, and how ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini answer the questions your buyers ask today, including which competitors they name.
2. **Prompt and keyword map.** We build one list covering the keywords you need to rank for and the prompts you need to be cited in.
3. **Fix the foundation.** Technical issues that block crawling, indexing or rendering are resolved first, because neither Google nor an AI crawler can use a page it cannot read.
4. **Restructure priority pages.** Each key page gets an answer-first opening, question-led headings, FAQs, comparison tables where they help, and matching schema.
5. **Build authority.** We earn backlinks and third-party mentions so that what your site says about you is corroborated elsewhere.
6. **Measure and iterate.** Rankings, traffic and AI citations are tracked monthly, and the next cycle is prioritized from that data.
